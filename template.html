      If your medicine is affected

      Start with your pharmacist — they can check other suppliers, order it in, and for the oestrogen patches can often supply an approved alternative without a new prescription. Filling your script a few days early, rather than on the last day, gives everyone room to move.

      About this site

      GAMSA publishes independent, referenced medicines information for trans and gender diverse people in South Australia. It is not affiliated with any clinic, pharmacy or manufacturer, and carries no advertising or sponsorship.

      Supply information is refreshed daily from the Therapeutic Goods Administration. Cost information is refreshed monthly from the Pharmaceutical Benefits Scheme. Clinical content is written and reviewed by hand, and every claim is referenced on the page it appears.

      This is health information, not medical advice. It cannot replace your prescriber or pharmacist, who know your history. Never change how you take a medicine based on this site alone. In an emergency call 000.

      Built and maintained by a registered pharmacist in Adelaide. An independent, non-commercial project, offered freely to South Australians.

      If your medicine is affected

      Start with your pharmacist — they can check other suppliers, order it in, and for the oestrogen patches can often supply an approved alternative without a new prescription. Filling your script a few days early, rather than on the last day, gives everyone room to move.

      Pharmacy stock reports

      Stock reports posted by registered pharmacists, newest first. Each pharmacist's registration number is checked against the AHPRA register before their first report is published; after that their reports appear straight away.

      Post a report

      For pharmacists and pharmacy staff posting on a pharmacist's behalf. Your registration number is stored but never shown; the pharmacy name and suburb are public.

      By posting you confirm you are a registered pharmacist, or are posting with one's permission, and that the report is accurate at the time of posting. Reports expire after 14 days.

      Send feedback

      Spotted something wrong, out of date or missing? Tell me here. Every message is read, and corrections are made as quickly as possible.

      Please do not include personal health details. Messages are stored privately and never shared, and no tracking cookies are used.
